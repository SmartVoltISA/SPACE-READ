# CONTRIBUTIONS — Human & AI Guide

## What this space is

`CONTRIBUTIONS/` is the external workspace for people, external LLMs and independent researchers working with the published SPACE architecture.

It is deliberately outside SPACE Core.

## Where to start

Read:

1. `../LLM_START.md` if you are an AI;
2. `../AI_INTERFACE.md`;
3. `../PUBLICATION_CONTRACT.md`;
4. `../READ_ONLY_POLICY.md`;
5. `CONTRIBUTION_TEMPLATE.md`.

## Where to put your work

Use:

`CONTRIBUTIONS/<identity>/<project-or-session>/`

Examples:

- `CONTRIBUTIONS/HUMAN/<name>/<project>/`
- `CONTRIBUTIONS/AI/<model>/<session>/`
- `CONTRIBUTIONS/RESEARCH/<group>/<project>/`
- `CONTRIBUTIONS/INDEPENDENT/<project>/`

Choose an identity that is clear and non-deceptive. Do not impersonate the SPACE project or another contributor.

## What you may leave here

- analysis;
- research journals;
- experiments;
- replications;
- critiques;
- counterexamples;
- implementation notes;
- proposals;
- questions;
- failed attempts;
- negative results;
- independent implementations.

## What you must not put here

Never publish:

- passwords;
- API keys;
- private keys;
- access tokens;
- secrets;
- confidential material;
- unnecessary personal data.

Do not copy private Core material into this public workspace unless it has already been explicitly approved for publication.

## What this space cannot do

A contribution does not:

- modify SPACE Core;
- modify Foundation;
- modify Guardian;
- become canonical state;
- become an accepted architecture change automatically.

The normal path is:

`CONTRIBUTION → REVIEW → VALIDATION / REPLICATION → ACCEPT / REJECT / HOLD`

## Recommended discipline

For substantial work, record:

- identity;
- date;
- SPACE-READ version/reference used;
- publication IDs used;
- objective;
- question or claim;
- evidence;
- method actually executed;
- observed result;
- interpretation separately;
- status;
- limitations;
- provenance;
- reproduction steps;
- recommendation;
- next question.

Use `CONTRIBUTION_TEMPLATE.md`.

## Simple rule

**SPACE-READ is what the project publishes.**

**CONTRIBUTIONS is what you discover, test, question or build around it.**

Keep the two separate.
