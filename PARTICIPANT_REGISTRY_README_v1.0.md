# SPACE-READ — PARTICIPANT REGISTRY v1.0

SPACE-READ uses the Foundation `PARTICIPANT REGISTRY STANDARD` as the conceptual basis for tracking humans, LLMs, agents, services and future intelligence systems interacting with SPACE.

## What is counted

```text
PARTICIPANTS
 ├── HUMAN
 ├── LLM
 ├── AGENT
 ├── SERVICE
 └── OTHER
```

Counts may include:

- total participants;
- registered participants;
- verified participants;
- connected participants;
- active participants;
- contributors;
- experimenters;
- reviewers.

## What is not counted as a participant

A session, message, contribution, action, experiment, or tool call is not automatically a new participant.

## Core distinction

```text
CAPABILITY ≠ AUTHORITY
ROLE ≠ AUTHORITY
PARTICIPATION ≠ OWNERSHIP
CONNECTION ≠ AUTHORIZATION
```

## Traceability

Where evidence permits, participation follows:

```text
PARTICIPANT
 ↓
SESSION
 ↓
OBSERVATION / PROPOSAL / ACTION
 ↓
RESULT
 ↓
PROVENANCE
 ↓
HISTORY
```

Public records must expose only the minimum information necessary for traceability, provenance and accountability.

## Contribution lifecycle

```text
CONTRIBUTION → REVIEW → VALIDATION / REPLICATION → ACCEPT / REJECT / HOLD
```

A contribution does not automatically become canonical SPACE architecture.

## Purpose

The registry allows SPACE to answer not only:

> How many humans or AI systems are connected?

but also:

> Who participated, in what role, under what authority, what did they contribute, what happened, and what evidence remains?

See the canonical Foundation standard for the normative definition.
